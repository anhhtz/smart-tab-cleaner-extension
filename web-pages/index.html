Workflow

How Smart Tab Cleaner Works

A seamless, non-intrusive background service worker that keeps your browser decluttered without manual effort.

1

Define Rules or Click Block

Open the extension popup to view recently visited sites or open tabs. Simply click Block or type custom Regular Expressions with your desired close delay.

2

Smart URL Match & Countdown

Whenever a tab finishes loading, the background worker evaluates the URL. If matched, the icon turns into a countdown timer badge while waiting for the timer to elapse.

3

Clean Tab & Multi-Device Sync

The target tab closes automatically. Your rules and settings immediately sync across all your laptops and desktops via Google's encrypted chrome.storage.sync.

Core Capabilities

Engineered for Productivity and Peace of Mind

Minimalist UI, maximum speed, and uncompromising respect for your privacy.

⚡

Fast Auto-Close Delay

Configure countdown delays down to 0ms for instant pop-up kills, or 500ms to allow external app launch protocols (e.g., desktop Zoom or Teams client) to trigger first.

🛡️

1-Click Quick Block

No need to manually type regex syntax. Browse recent history or active tabs right in the popup and click "Block" to automatically extract clean domain rules.

☁️

Google Cloud Sync

Full support for Chrome's native chrome.storage.sync. Configure your rules once at work and have them seamlessly apply to your personal computer.

🎯

Regex Matcher & Tester

Includes a built-in interactive URL pattern tester. Verify that your regex pattern properly triggers or avoids false positives before committing changes.

🔒

Zero Tracking & Local Only

We never transmit, log, or monetize your browsing history. Everything executes locally in memory on your computer, with zero third-party telemetry.

📥

JSON Export & Import

Take full ownership of your rules. Export your complete rule configurations to JSON anytime and restore or share them across teams in seconds.

🛡️

Compliant with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Policies

Smart Tab Cleaner strictly follows the Single Purpose policy and operates exclusively on-device. We do not sell user data, inject advertisements, or use external tracking servers.
