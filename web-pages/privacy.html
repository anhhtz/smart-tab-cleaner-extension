Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 • Extension: Smart Tab Cleaner • Developer: anhhtz

Summary: Smart Tab Cleaner is built on a 100% Client-Side, Privacy-First architecture. We do NOT collect, track, log, sell, or transmit any of your personal data, browsing history, or search queries to any third-party servers. All rule matching and tab management happen locally on your computer.

1. Introduction & Single Purpose Statement

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Smart Tab Cleaner browser extension ("the Extension", "we", "our") handles user information and permissions.

In accordance with the Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, Smart Tab Cleaner is committed to a clear Single Purpose: Automated tab lifecycle management — automatically detecting and closing user-designated clutter tabs (such as pop-up advertisements, spam redirect links, and disposable app launchers like Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Telegram) according to customizable rules and countdown timers.

2. Data Collection & Limited Use Disclosure

We strictly practice Data Minimization. Under no circumstances do we collect, process, or transmit Personally Identifiable Information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, IP address, geolocation, or hardware identifiers.
  • No Remote Tracking or Telemetry: We do not integrate any third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude), crash reporters, or tracking beacons.
  • No Sale or Transfer of Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your browsing information to brokers, advertisers, or third parties.
  • No Advertising: The Extension does not inject or serve advertisements into web pages or within the extension interface.

3. Chrome Permissions & Technical Justification

To deliver its core tab-closing capabilities, Smart Tab Cleaner requests specific browser permissions in its manifest.json. Each permission is strictly used for its declared local purpose:

Permission Why It Is Needed How Data Is Handled
tabs Required to inspect the URL of newly completed browser tabs in real-time, determine whether they match an active rule, and invoke chrome.tabs.remove() after the countdown delay. Also used to populate the list of currently open tabs in the popup for the Quick Block feature. Processed 100% in local memory. URLs are evaluated against local regex patterns and immediately discarded. URLs are never written to permanent disk logs, external servers, or remote endpoints.
history Required to query recently visited websites solely when the user opens the extension popup, enabling the convenient "Quick Block from History" feature so users can block annoying sites without having to reopen them. Queried on-demand and kept local. History items are fetched temporarily into memory only while the popup is open. History items are never uploaded or transferred anywhere.
storage Required to save your configured rules, blacklist patterns, and countdown settings. Data is stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local and optionally synchronized across your devices via Google's official chrome.storage.sync API linked to your signed-in Google Account.

4. Cloud Synchronization (Google Chrome Sync)

Smart Tab Cleaner provides cross-device synchronization using Chrome's native chrome.storage.sync API.

  • Data synchronized consists exclusively of user-created rule sets (e.g., rule names, regex patterns, enabled states) and configuration values (default timeout in milliseconds).
  • This synchronization is entirely managed and secured by Google using the security infrastructure of your Google Account. We do not own, maintain, or operate any intermediary synchronization servers.
  • If you do not sign in to a Google Account or disable Chrome Sync, all configurations remain strictly confined to your local device.

5. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

You maintain full sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • Manual Management: You can view, add, modify, or delete any individual rule or setting through the extension popup.
  • JSON Export / Import: You can export your entire rule database to a standard JSON file at any time for backup or inspection.
  • Complete Deletion: Removing or uninstalling Smart Tab Cleaner from your Chrome browser immediately and permanently purges all local storage and synced rules associated with the extension.

6. Third-Party Websites & External Links

Smart Tab Cleaner interacts with third-party URLs exclusively by reading their address string to determine if a rule match occurs. The Extension does not inject content scripts into page DOMs, does not modify website content, and does not read web page cookies, passwords, or form inputs.

7. Compliance with Global Privacy Regulations

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU)

Because Smart Tab Cleaner does not collect, process, or store personal data on any server, the developer acts neither as a Data Controller nor as a Data Processor under the GDPR. All operations remain entirely within the user's localized browser boundary.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

We do not collect consumer personal information, nor do we sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Smart Tab Cleaner is safe for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications to extension features, improvements in browser security policies, or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
